## 2. Skill Integration into Workflow Stages

1. **Stage 1 (Architecture & GDD Alignment):** `agency-game-designer` and `karpathy-guidelines` ensure all additions enrich player satisfaction without adding structural bloat or breaking existing save schemas.
2. **Stage 2 & 3 (Environments, Candy Rendering & VFX):** `agency-ui-designer` and `agency-performance-benchmarker` guide layered procedural parallax drawing and object-pooled particle emitters.
3. **Stage 4 & 5 (Character Expressiveness & Physics Engine):** `agency-game-designer` governs anticipation and reward pacing, while `test-driven-development` enforces 1:1 mathematical equivalence between trajectory dots and projectile velocity.
4. **Stage 6 (Audio & Haptics Engine):** Multi-bus gain staging and Web Audio procedural synthesis designed for low latency.
5. **Stage 7 (Commercial-Quality UI/UX):** `agency-ui-designer` and `agency-frontend-developer` deliver an animated Home screen, interactive 3-step tutorial, volume sliders, and clean HUD action bar.
6. **Stage 8 & 9 (QA, Performance & Verification):** `agency-performance-benchmarker` and `verification-before-completion` execute automated suites, Playwright headless Chrome automation across 6 viewports, and generate audit-grade reports.
